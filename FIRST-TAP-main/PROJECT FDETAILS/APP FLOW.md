Application Overview  This document defines the complete end-to-end user journey, navigation flow, conversation flow, lesson flow, state transitions, and AI interaction sequence for the FirstTap Hackathon MVP.  The application follows a **Voice-First Learning Model**, where users interact naturally with an AI Digital Mentor instead of navigating through traditional menus and buttons. Every screen transition is guided by the AI mentor, creating an experience similar to learning from a patient human teacher.  The MVP demonstrates one complete learning journey for **Google Pay Digital Payment in Tamil**, while establishing a reusable application flow for future lessons such as DigiLocker, WhatsApp, Banking, Government Services, Healthcare, and other digital services.  ---  # 2. Complete User Journey  ```  Launch Application          │          ▼  Splash Screen          │          ▼  Avatar Greeting          │          ▼  Voice Lesson Selection          │          ▼  Google Pay Lesson Introduction          │          ▼  Guided Demonstration          │          ▼  Understanding Check          │          ▼  Interactive Practice          │          ▼  AI Mentor Assistance          │          ▼  Doubt Clearing Session          │          ▼  Digital Confidence Assessment          │          ▼  Completion & Next Lesson Suggestion  ```  This flow represents the complete learning cycle from onboarding to confidence assessment.  ---  # 3. Screen Navigation Flow  ## Splash Screen  Purpose:  - Display FirstTap branding.  - Load application assets.  - Initialize AI services.  - Prepare voice recognition and speech synthesis.  Transition:  Automatically navigates to the Avatar Greeting after initialization.  ---  ## Avatar Greeting  The AI Mentor welcomes the learner in Tamil.  Example:  "வணக்கம்! நான் FirstTap. இன்று நீங்கள் என்ன கற்றுக்கொள்ள விரும்புகிறீர்கள்?"  Actions:  - Avatar performs a welcoming animation.  - Speech and subtitles are synchronized.  - Microphone automatically activates after greeting.  Transition:  Moves to Voice Lesson Selection.  ---  ## Voice Lesson Selection  The learner responds naturally.  Example:  "Google Pay கற்றுக்கொள்ள வேண்டும்."  The AI:  - Identifies the lesson intent.  - Confirms the selected lesson.  - Introduces the learning session.  Transition:  Moves directly to Lesson Introduction.  ---  ## Lesson Introduction  The mentor explains:  - What Google Pay is.  - What the learner will accomplish.  - Reassures the learner that no real money will be used.  Transition:  Automatically begins the Guided Demonstration.  ---  ## Guided Demonstration  The demonstration is fully automated.  For each lesson step:  1. Highlight the UI element.  2. Avatar explains the action.  3. Display synchronized subtitles.  4. Animate the cursor/finger interaction.  5. Update the phone simulator.  6. Pause briefly.  7. Continue automatically.  No manual "Next" button is provided.  Lesson Flow:  Home Screen  ↓  Open Google Pay  ↓  Enter App PIN  ↓  Select Contact  ↓  Enter Amount  ↓  Tap Pay  ↓  Enter UPI PIN  ↓  Payment Processing  ↓  Payment Successful  ↓  Receipt Display  The learner observes the complete transaction before attempting it independently.  ---  # 4. Learning Interaction Flow  ## Understanding Check  After the demonstration, the mentor asks a few simple questions to verify understanding.  Example:  - Which button starts the payment?  - When should the UPI PIN be entered?  - What appears after a successful payment?  The learner answers through voice.  The mentor provides immediate feedback and clarification if required.  Transition:  Moves to Practice Mode.  ---  ## Practice Mode  The learner now performs the same Google Pay transaction inside a completely simulated environment.  The learner interacts directly with the phone simulator.  The AI mentor continuously observes:  - Incorrect button selections.  - Long inactivity (e.g., 10 seconds).  - Repeated mistakes.  - User hesitation.  When assistance is required, the mentor:  - Explains the correct action.  - Highlights the correct button.  - Encourages the learner.  - Allows another attempt.  No real payment or banking service is involved.  ---  ## Doubt Clearing Session  After completing the practice session, the learner can ask questions naturally.  Examples:  - What is a UPI PIN?  - What happens if I enter the wrong PIN?  - Can I cancel a payment?  - What is a QR Code?  The AI uses the current lesson context to generate relevant answers.  This conversational phase continues until the learner indicates completion.  ---  ## Digital Confidence Assessment (DCA)  The application evaluates the learner's progress based on:  - Lesson completion.  - Practice completion.  - Mistakes corrected.  - Number of hints used.  - Questions asked.  - Overall participation.  The learner receives:  - Digital Confidence Score.  - Personalized encouragement.  - Recommendation for the next lesson.  Transition:  Moves to Completion Screen.  ---  # 5. Conversation State Flow  The application operates using a controlled conversation state machine.  ```  IDLE  ↓  GREETING  ↓  LISTENING  ↓  THINKING  ↓  SPEAKING  ↓  LESSON_INTRODUCTION  ↓  LESSON_RUNNING  ↓  UNDERSTANDING_CHECK  ↓  PRACTICE  ↓  DOUBT_CLEARING  ↓  ASSESSMENT  ↓  LESSON_COMPLETE  ```  Only one conversation state is active at any given time, ensuring predictable behavior and smooth transitions.  ---  # 6. Voice Interaction Flow  FirstTap follows a continuous voice interaction cycle.  ```  Avatar Speaks          │          ▼  Speech Ends          │          ▼  Automatic Listening          │          ▼  Speech Recognition          │          ▼  Intent Detection          │          ▼  Gemini Response          │          ▼  Speech Synthesis          │          ▼  Avatar Speaks Again  ```  The microphone remains automatically managed by the system, minimizing manual interaction and creating a natural conversational experience.  ---  # 7. Error & Edge Case Handling  The application gracefully handles common scenarios.  - If no speech is detected, the mentor gently prompts the learner again.  - If the learner goes off-topic, the AI redirects the conversation back to the current lesson.  - If speech synthesis is unavailable, subtitles continue while the lesson proceeds.  - During the Guided Demonstration, voice interruptions are ignored to preserve lesson continuity.  - During Practice Mode and Doubt Clearing, voice interaction remains fully enabled.  These safeguards ensure a stable and user-friendly learning experience.  ---  # 8. Future Application Flow  The current Google Pay lesson serves as the blueprint for all future lessons.  Additional services such as DigiLocker, WhatsApp, Banking, Government Services, Healthcare, Online Shopping, and other digital platforms will reuse the same application flow by replacing only the lesson configuration files.  This standardized flow enables FirstTap to scale into a comprehensive multilingual digital literacy platform without redesigning the core application architecture.   `
