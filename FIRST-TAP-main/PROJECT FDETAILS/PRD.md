Product Requirements Document (PRD)
===================================

FirstTap – AI-Powered Multilingual Digital Mentor
-------------------------------------------------

### Version 1.0 | Hackathon MVP Specification

1\. Product Overview
====================

Product Name
------------

**FirstTap – Your First Step Towards Digital Confidence**

Product Vision
--------------

FirstTap is an AI-powered, voice-first digital mentor designed to help first-time smartphone users confidently learn and practice essential digital services through interactive simulations, real-time AI guidance, and personalized mentoring in their native language.

Unlike conventional video tutorials, documentation, or chatbot-based assistants, FirstTap creates an immersive learning environment where users learn by observing guided demonstrations, practicing safely in simulated environments, asking questions naturally through voice, and receiving instant assistance from an AI mentor.

The Hackathon MVP demonstrates one complete learning journey: **Google Pay Digital Payment in Tamil**, while establishing a scalable architecture capable of supporting multiple services and regional languages.

2\. Problem Statement
=====================

India is rapidly adopting digital services such as UPI payments, DigiLocker, online banking, WhatsApp, and various government portals. However, millions of elderly citizens, first-time smartphone users, rural populations, and digitally inexperienced individuals struggle to adopt these services due to several barriers.

Major challenges include:

*   Language barriers caused by English-centric interfaces.
    
*   Fear of making irreversible mistakes during digital transactions.
    
*   Lack of personalized guidance while learning.
    
*   Dependence on family members for routine digital tasks.
    
*   Inability to practice safely before using real applications.
    
*   Low digital confidence resulting in avoidance of digital services.
    

Existing learning methods—including YouTube tutorials, user manuals, and customer support—are passive and do not allow users to interact, ask questions, or build confidence through guided practice.

3\. Proposed Solution
=====================

FirstTap addresses these challenges by introducing an AI-powered digital mentor that teaches users through a structured, voice-driven learning experience.

The application combines:

*   Human-like AI Mentor
    
*   Voice-first interaction
    
*   Interactive smartphone simulation
    
*   Guided demonstrations
    
*   Safe practice sandbox
    
*   Real-time doubt clearing
    
*   Personalized progress assessment
    

The AI mentor behaves like a patient teacher rather than a chatbot, guiding users step-by-step, answering questions contextually, correcting mistakes positively, and encouraging learners throughout the experience.

======================

The primary objectives of FirstTap are:

The MVP specifically aims to demonstrate that one complete lesson can be delivered through a fully voice-controlled AI mentor with guided demonstration and interactive practice.

6\. Hackathon MVP Scope
=======================

The Round-1 prototype focuses on a single digital service:

Google Pay Learning Journey (Tamil)
-----------------------------------

The complete learning flow consists of:

1.  Splash Screen
    
2.  AI Avatar Greeting
    
3.  Voice-based lesson selection
    
4.  Automated Google Pay demonstration
    
5.  Short understanding check
    
6.  Interactive practice sandbox
    
7.  AI-assisted mistake correction
    
8.  Doubt clearing conversation
    
9.  Digital Confidence Assessment (DCA)
    
10.  Completion and encouragement
    

The MVP intentionally limits itself to one lesson to demonstrate the platform's architecture and user experience.

7\. Core Features
=================

### AI Digital Mentor

*   Human-like South Indian female avatar
    
*   Friendly conversational behavior
    
*   Continuous voice interaction
    
*   Regional language communication
    

### Voice-First Experience

*   Automatic greeting
    
*   Always-listening interaction where appropriate
    
*   Speech recognition
    
*   Speech synthesis
    
*   Minimal dependence on buttons
    

### Guided Demonstration

*   Fully automated lesson
    
*   Step-by-step narration
    
*   Smartphone simulator
    
*   Animated cursor guidance
    
*   Highlighted interactions
    
*   No manual "Next" navigation
    

### Practice Sandbox

*   Safe simulated environment
    
*   No real financial transactions
    
*   Interactive learning
    
*   AI guidance during mistakes
    
*   Real-time assistance
    

### Doubt Clearing

*   Natural voice conversation
    
*   Context-aware AI responses
    
*   Lesson-specific explanations
    
*   Continuous learning support
    

### Digital Confidence Assessment

The learner receives a Digital Confidence Score based on:

*   Lesson completion
    
*   Practice performance
    
*   Number of hints used
    
*   Mistakes corrected
    
*   Questions asked
    
*   Overall participation

*   A complete voice-driven Google Pay learning journey.
    
*   Smooth AI mentor interaction.
    
*   Automated guided demonstration.
    
*   Interactive practice environment.

The prototype should clearly communicate that FirstTap is not merely a tutorial application but an intelligent digital learning platform capable of transforming first-time smartphone users into digitally confident citizens.

10\. Product Differentiators
============================

FirstTap differentiates itself through five core innovations:

*   **Voice-First Learning:** Users interact primarily through natural speech instead of traditional menus.
    
*   **Learn by Doing:** Interactive demonstrations combined with safe hands-on practice.
    
*   **AI Mentor:** A conversational digital companion that guides, teaches, and encourages learners.
    
*   **Safe Practice Sandbox:** Users build confidence without the risk of real-world mistakes.
    
*   **Digital Confidence Assessment:** Progress is measured to motivate continuous learning rather than simply completing lessons.
    

These differentiators position FirstTap as a scalable AI-powered digital literacy platform capable of supporting India's broader digital inclusion initiatives while delivering an engaging, accessible, and personalized learning experience.
